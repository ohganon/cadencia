Scope: Cadencia style board (docs/fase6/tablero/index.html). Experience mode: the game world leads. Audience: players aged 15–55 on phones. Job: the client approves the visual world before the build starts in Godot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every level is a town band's marching drill chart. The chain is a formation that advances one tile per beat, and the bells are the notes. This refuses the category default of glossy candy toys with gold frames.

OWN-WORLD: the ground is scarlet uniform cloth (#c8202f) and wool navy (#16213f). Accents are polished brass (#d9a93b) and white chalk. Chalk grids with measure numbers. Tiles are scarlet with a brass top edge; bells are brass noteheads with a stem. Bodoni Moda caps play the band programme; Atkinson Hyperlegible carries the UI.

STORY: the visitor sees that path length is time, that the formation falls to the beat, and that Villa Cadencia is a town band.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a phone at the centre of the board, showing a navy drill field at full size with a playable chain counting 1, 2, 3… towards a double bell. Brass count numbers, the programme title in Bodoni, and the "¡Ya!" baton as the primary action at the bottom of the phone.

FORM: assigned direction, position 7 on the grounded list. seed:2230e787e94c1023. Raises: scale-only hierarchy, a single time grid, unmistakable chain states, one brightest focus, and promise first with rules on the back.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
